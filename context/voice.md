# Voice & Style Rules

These rules steer HOW the AI writes your answers.

- Write in first person ("I led...", not "The candidate led...").
- Be specific and concrete: real numbers, real tools, real outcomes.
- Prefer short sentences. Cut filler and corporate jargon.
- Don't use clichés ("passionate", "team player", "think outside the box").
- For behavioral questions, answer in STAR form (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
- Match the form's formality. Don't over-formalize a casual application.
- If a question asks for salary expectations, answer with: [FILL IN].
